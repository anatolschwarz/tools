**Step-by-Step Plan Execution**

We are going to implement that plan step-by-step.

- Present each step literally as it is in the plan.
- Explain what it is about.
- Wait for step execution approval.
- Upon approval, execute.
- Present the results.
- State if success or failure.
- On success, present literally the next step, and so on.
- On failure, describe briefly the reason and Wait.
